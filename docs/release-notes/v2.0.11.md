## mabi-patcher 2.0.11

### New
- **Sync with the uotiara installer** (Mods › Online, on by default). mabi-patcher reads the uotiara installer's registry record. It installs every website mod ticked there that is missing from `mods/web/`. Removing a mod in mabi-patcher unticks it in the uotiara installer, and installing one ticks it. Changing those ticks needs administrator rights. A status line shows the installed uotiara version, the latest release on GitHub and how many mods are ticked.

### Fixed
- **"Session expired" during Nexon maintenance.** During maintenance Nexon refuses saved sessions, and mabi-patcher reported them as expired: 3 log lines plus a re-login dialog. When Nexon's news feed has an open maintenance notice, the session is now kept and one line explains it, for example "Mabinogi is under maintenance until Oct 22, 9:00 AM. Your saved session was kept; it works again after maintenance."
- **Maintenance shown as "No" during maintenance.** Nexon's maintenance check only answers for a valid session. The Patcher now also reads the maintenance notice and shows "Yes, until <time>" with the estimated end in your local time.
- **One message for an expired session.** A session that really expired now logs one line and opens one re-login dialog.
- **Logged-in clock after CLI, MCP and Kanan logins.** Logins and imports from those paths now restart the "signed in for" time.
- **Email sessions with no known expiry renew.** They now renew 6 hours after the last login while the app runs. Before, they never renewed.
