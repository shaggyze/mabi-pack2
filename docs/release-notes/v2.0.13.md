## mabi-patcher 2.0.13

### New
- **Minimize mabi-patcher to tray on Launch.** A new Launcher setting hides the window to the tray after the game starts. The tray icon stays until the window is opened again.
- **Settings reorganized.** Launch Mode, the launch toggles and the Before/After Launch hooks moved to the Launcher tab. Update checks, mod auto update, Start with Windows and Run as Administrator moved to Autostart. The Patcher tab keeps the game file settings and the patch hooks.

### Fixed
- **Game archive rebuilds after a uotiara update.** When the uotiara installer replaces `package\uotiara_00001.it` or its loose files change, startup rebuilds the archive with your installed mods before the game launches. A rebuild no longer rolls back to the older `.bak`. The state is kept in `mods\.cache\installed.sig`.
- **Corrupt mods are skipped.** Each mod is decoded once and checked before it is copied into the game archive. A mod with a bad entry is skipped and the others install.
- **No default blue flash at startup.** The window opens once, after the self-update, in your saved theme and background color.
- **Tooltips.** A label and its switch now show the same tooltip. Tooltips are no longer cut off at the screen edge when the sidebar is collapsed, and over the Browser tab they use the system tooltip.
- **Clearer sync log lines.** "Community sync OK" and the website sync line say they count your own uploads and your website account picks. They do not count your installed mods.
